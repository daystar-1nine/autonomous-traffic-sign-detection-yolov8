🚗 Traffic Signs Test Gallery

Is page ko laptop par open rakhein aur phone ka rear camera screen ke samne karein test karne ke liye!


1. STOP SIGN
Action: Gaadi turant brake lagakar rukegi (Emergency / Regulatory Stop).

2. LEFT TURN
Action: Gaadi left wheel slow karke baayein mudegi.

3. STRAIGHT AHEAD
Action: Gaadi seedhe standard speed par aage chalegi.

4. RIGHT TURN
Action: Gaadi right wheel slow karke daayein mudegi.

5. NO ENTRY
Action: Obstacle warning - Gaadi aage nahi jaayegi, ruk jayegi.

6. DEAD END
Action: Rasta band hone par gaadi stop karegi.
