# 02 - Cosmic Byte Ares Pro Gamepad Hardware & Setup Guide

---

## 1. Purpose
This document details the hardware specifications, charging, operating modes, and Android preparation for the **Cosmic Byte Ares Pro** wireless gamepad.

---

## 2. Requirements
- 1x Cosmic Byte Ares Pro Gamepad.
- USB Type-C charging cable.
- Android smartphone with Bluetooth 4.2+ (Bluetooth 5.0 recommended).

---

## 3. Hardware Specifications
- **Connectivity:** Bluetooth 5.0, 2.4GHz Wireless USB Dongle, Type-C Wired.
- **Battery:** 1000mAh Rechargeable Lithium Polymer (12–15 hours runtime).
- **Vibration:** Dual Haptic Feedback Motors.
- **Sensors:** Analog hall-effect triggers, 360° precision analog thumbsticks.
- **Supported Platforms:** Android, iOS, Windows PC, Nintendo Switch.

---

## 4. Setup Steps
1. Fully charge the gamepad using the USB Type-C cable until the LED indicators turn solid.
2. Unplug the 2.4GHz USB receiver if plugged into another computer to prevent auto-reconnection.
3. Power on the controller by holding the **Home button** for 2 seconds.
4. Verify all physical buttons click smoothly and the analog sticks spring cleanly back to center.

---

## 5. Expected Output
- Controller LEDs light up in scanning sequence.
- Thumbsticks exhibit zero mechanical binding.

---

## 6. Common Errors & Troubleshooting
| Symptom | Cause | Solution |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Controller powers off immediately | Low battery | Charge for at least 60 minutes before pairing. |
| Stick drift on idle | Mechanical calibration off | Power off controller, rotate sticks 3 times, power on. |

---

## 7. Verification Checklist
- [ ] Battery charged above 80%.
- [ ] Controller powers on cleanly into pairing mode.
- [ ] Left stick springs back to exact (0, 0) center position.
